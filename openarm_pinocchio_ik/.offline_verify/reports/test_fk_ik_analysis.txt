  - model.lower: Lower position limits (rad)
  - model.upper: Upper position limits (rad)

Error Calculation:
  - Position error: Euclidean norm of position difference
  - Orientation error: Rotation angle from relative rotation matrix
  - Formula: acos((trace(R^T * R) - 1) / 2)
